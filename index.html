05 / JOB SEARCH SYSTEM

Make your work
easy to discover.

01

Target the right titles

Search for IT Operations Analyst, ITSM Analyst, ServiceNow Analyst, IT Support Analyst, Application Support Analyst, and Reporting Analyst roles. Treat Business Systems Analyst as a parallel path after you build requirements and process-mapping evidence.

02

Use a weekly rhythm

Each week: 8–12 tailored applications, 3–5 relevant networking messages, one portfolio improvement, and one mock interview. Prioritize fit and quality over mass applying.

03

Show evidence everywhere

Add your website to LinkedIn and your résumé. Pin your best project repository. Share a short post explaining one insight, challenge, or lesson from each completed project.

Measure the funnel

Track role, company, date, résumé version, contact, status, and follow-up date. Review responses every two weeks and adjust keywords, targeting, or project evidence based on results.
